# Series

Every reel belongs to one series. A series gives viewers a reason to follow (they know
what the next one will be) and gives each reel a ready-made hook shape, honesty beat and
look. Three series run in rotation; post them A, B, C, A, B, C so no two in a row share a
series. The topic backlog is `TOPICS.md`.

All rules in `CLAUDE.md` and `QUALITY.md` apply to every series. On-screen series names are
English; the spoken line is Hinglish.

## A. Viral Claim Check

The channel's signature: a claim people forward, checked by hand on camera.

- **Done:** Yawm 365.
- **Hook shape:** the claim itself, stated as the post states it, with the number inside
  the first 1.5 s ("Samandar 32 baar, zameen 13 baar?").
- **Spine:** the count or source, shown step by step → under which rule the claim holds
  or breaks → what is actually remarkable. One claim per reel unless two share one idea;
  worded fresh each time (no stock "maine khud check kiya" line).
- **Honesty beat:** what the claim gets wrong and what it gets right. Never a sneer at
  the people who shared it.
- **On screen:** a `VIRAL CLAIM` tag on the post; a verdict stamp at the payoff
  (`HOLDS ONLY IF…`, `FALSE`, `TRUE, BUT…`); the ledger look (counting, rules as rows).
- **Claim labels:** numerical observation, established science, interpretation.

## B. Forgotten History

Muslim history the viewer has never heard, tied to something in their life today.

- **Done:** none yet.
- **Hook shape:** start from an object or word the viewer owns now ("Tumhari jeb ka
  rupaya…", "Hospital mein free ilaaj…", "Ye algorithm…"), or from a scene at its
  most tense moment ("1258. Baghdad. Darya ka rang kaala ho gaya."). The person or place
  comes second.
- **Spine:** one scene, one person, one object. No timelines of dynasties.
- **Honesty beat:** record versus later story. Say which details come from a chronicle of
  the time and which were written centuries later; say who calls something "the first"
  or "the oldest", and who disagrees.
- **On screen:** Seerah-Atlas-style maps (3D relief, drawn routes, gold place names),
  archival photos and manuscripts as black-and-white cut-outs on one bold colour
  (yevorqq collage), parchment for quotations with a source line, years shown as both CE
  and AH.
- **Never:**
  - Depicting the Prophets or the Companions.
  - AI-generated people or places presented as real.
  - Film stills (copyright).
  - Reconstructions, when used, are tagged `RECONSTRUCTION`.
- **Claim labels:** historical record, later report, legend, scholarly view, disputed.
- **References:** `references/seerah-atlas-badr`, `references/yevorqq-ai-websites`,
  `references/nitish-rajput-black-box` (one continuous 3D world).

## C. Quran ke Lafz

One word, line or pattern of the Quran looked at closely.

- **Done:** Kullun fi Falak (and the earlier Quran Facts reel).
- **Hook shape:** the surprising property of the word or line, shown on the word itself
  in the first frame.
- **Spine:** the Ayah (fetched, never typed) → the word → what a nearby word would have
  meant instead → what the Tafsir says.
- **Honesty beat:** linguistic observation, not proof; whose Tafsir; where scholars
  differ.
- **On screen:** the Ayah on a torn paper card with an orange Surah:Ayah tag, the word
  ringed by hand, the meaning beside it; the word not chosen shown and crossed out;
  macro or nature B-roll for concrete nouns.
- **Claim labels:** Quranic context, interpretation, linguistic observation.
- **References:** `references/towards-eternity-ant-crush`,
  `references/towards-eternity-machhar`.
