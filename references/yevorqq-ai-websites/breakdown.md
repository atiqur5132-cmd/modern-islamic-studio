## Hook, 0–6 s

- **Frame 0:** the speaker, mid-shot, eyes in the upper third, with "NOBODY BELIEVES ME" in
  heavy condensed white caps across the chest. The line itself is the hook: it opens a
  gap ("believes what?"). It arrives with a zoom-out plus blur-to-sharp, cubic-out.
- **0.0–1.7 s:** speaker only. Small words of the rest of the sentence ("when I tell them
  that") appear under the big line in a plain small font: one big line, the rest small.
- **About 1.7 s:** a whip with heavy motion blur into a full-screen collage, on the words
  "$10,000 a month".
- **Collage (1.8–4.7 s):** warm grey paper wall with window-light shadows, a big yellow sun
  disc, dollar bills hanging on fishing hooks dropping in from different directions, a
  black-and-white cut-out man in a suit with an old CRT monitor for a head, arms crossed.
  An orange number counts up to "$10000" (2.6–3.3) with "a month" in a serif below and a

- **4.8 s:** hard change of world on "selling AI websites": saturated royal-blue ground,

## Captions and on-screen text

- No running caption track. Text is kinetic and placed per line, beside or across the
  speaker: key words big and heavy, filler words small ("LET / ME be with / CLEAR /
  YOU"), stacked to the side of the face.
- Numbers get colour and glow: "$1.4 MILLION" in bright green with a glow, under
  "I'VE MADE" in white.
- Letter-scramble reveal and exit ("AI WEBSITES TO LOCAL BUSINESSES" breaks into letters).
- Three fonts by role: heavy condensed caps (spoken words), a small plain sans (filler
  and labels), a chunky serif (full-screen titles).

## Take / leave for our channel

- **Take:**
  - The hook line opens a gap on frame 0 in huge type. The full-screen picture of the
    claim arrives by about 1.7 s, and a new world arrives about every 1.5–3 s.
  - One full-screen scene per spoken phrase, each in its own colour world.
  - Black-and-white cut-out collage (paper ground, real cut-out photos, one bold colour
    disc or ground, one accent for the number). It is cheap, strong at thumbnail size,
    and reads as designed, not as AI. For us: archival or public-domain photos of
    manuscripts, astrolabes, desert and stars as cut-outs, with real images
    (visual-sourcing rules apply).
  - Kinetic text with big key words and small filler words, beside the face, instead of
    an even caption band, at least for the hook and key lines.
  - A riser through the hook, dropping on the first payoff word, with a boom when it
    returns to the speaker.
  - A year or number ruler that scrolls for time spans and counts.
- **Leave:**
  - Money imagery and hype ("nobody believes me").
  - A glow on everything: we keep one accent with meaning.
  - Fishing hooks and monitor-head surrealism: fine for a business reel, but wrong in
    tone for Quranic content unless the subject calls for it.
  - Letter-scramble on Quranic or Arabic text: never.
- **Against our hook rule:** this reel opens on the speaker (0–1.7 s), not on graphics,
  but with a huge hook line, motion on the text, and a full-screen visual by 1.7 s. It
  supports the A/B test idea: face plus big line for 1.5 s, then full-screen, versus
  graphics first.
