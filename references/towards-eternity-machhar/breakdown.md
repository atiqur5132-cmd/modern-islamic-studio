# Towards Eternity: "Quran mein machhar ka zikr kyun kiya gaya hai?" (Hindi/Urdu)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U0Pu2bm0VuA (Towards Eternity; 156K views when
studied). 69 s, 1080×1920. Same presenter and set as the "ant" reel, with Hindi/Urdu audio
and Urdu-script captions: a channel version for our audience.
Studied 2026-11-06 at 1080p: frames every 0.5 s to 9 s, every 2.5 s after, two full frames.
Pictures on the laptop only.
