# Seerah Atlas: "At Badr, Each Army Saw the Other as Few. Why?"

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p2PjoAuEVPo (Seerah Atlas; only 2.5K views when
studied, so this is a style reference, not proof that it works). 87 s, 1080×1920.
Studied 2026-11-06 at 1080p: frames every 0.5 s to 9 s, every 3 s after, two full frames.
Pictures on the laptop only.

Faceless Seerah storytelling with no people depicted: armies are swarms of light, so
there are no images of the Prophet ﷺ or the Companions.

## Hook, 0–6 s (hook.png)

- Beats at 0.0 (low drone flight over 3D desert dunes, warm light), about 2.8 (the first
  army appears as a glowing swarm), about 3.5 (the second swarm), then the camera keeps
  moving. Three or four beats; the picture never stops, but nothing new arrives in the
  first 2.8 s.
- Line: "At Badr, the Quran says…", "what two armies…", with the yellow key word.

## Graphic vocabulary (sheet.jpg)

| Line type | What they show | Hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place | 3D relief terrain, real coastline (Red Sea), the place name in gold serif ("BADR") | 3 s |
| Route | A red line drawing itself across the terrain (Makkah → Badr); a dotted caravan route | 3–6 s |
| Armies or numbers | Swarms of glowing particles on the ground; the number above ("1,000") | 3 s |
| Time or omen | Sun and sky shots (sunrise over sand, two suns as a dream image) | 3 s |
| A quote | An aged parchment card on a dark ground, text in serif | 3–12 s |

## Captions

Bold condensed caps, white with the key word in yellow, 2–3 words, low-middle.

## Take / leave

- **Take:**
  - People as particles, places as 3D relief, routes as drawn red lines. It is the right
    answer for Seerah and history topics without depicting anyone, and it is buildable
    in our Remotion 3D.
  - Gold serif place names.
  - Parchment for quotes, with the source tag our rules need.
- **Leave:**
  - Starting on 2.8 s of empty dunes (it fails our hook gate).
  - Long 12 s parchment holds.
  - The low view count means the hook is not proven.
