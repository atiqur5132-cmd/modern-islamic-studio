# Aashqeen Plus: "Muslims billi paalte hain, par dogs kyun nahi" (Hindi)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EkpC96dModg (Aashqeen Plus; 3.8M views when
studied). 67 s, 1080×1920. A Hindi Islamic explainer for a mixed audience. The same
channel's "Muslims Mecca kyun jaate hain" (2.4M) uses the same grammar.
Studied 2026-11-06 at 1080p: frames every 0.5 s to 9 s, every 2.5 s after, two full frames.
Pictures on the laptop only.

## Hook, 0–6 s (hook.png)

- **Frame 0 is the thumbnail itself:** a designed poster ("MUSLIMS KE GHAR", cat with a
  green tick, dog with a red cross, the presenter in a cap in the middle). Whoever
  clicked the thumbnail sees it continue; whoever scrolls past sees a poster, not a face.
- **Two characters, one person:** the questioner (black T-shirt, warm orange/green
  backdrop) asks, the explainer (white collar shirt, green backdrop, holding a real cat)
  answers. Cutting between them is a cut every 1–2 s that also changes the costume, the
  colour and the framing.
- Beats at 0.0 (poster), about 1.0 (questioner, with a cut-out of five puppies in front of
  his chest), 1.5 ("Nafrat" in giant yellow, "Kyun karte ho" in white script below),
  2.5 (explainer with the cat, "Nahi To!" in big white), 3.5 (small caption), 5.0
  (questioner again). Six beats; longest still about 1.5 s.

## Captions and text

- Mostly no caption track. Only the key word is on screen, huge: yellow for the charged
  word, white for the retort, a small script font for the rest of the phrase.
- Over B-roll, one word ("Saliva", "Impure", "Not Allowed", "Farming") in yellow, often
  on a black-and-white photo with red tones.

## Graphic vocabulary

| Line type | What they show | Hold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The question | Cut-out objects in front of the speaker (puppies), the charged word in giant yellow | 1–1.5 s |
| A rule | One word over a desaturated or red-tinted photo | 2 s |
| Real practice | Real footage: wudu water, congregation praying, a dog on a farm | 2–2.5 s |

## Take / leave

- **Take:**
  - Frame 0 = the thumbnail poster: our cover thumbnail can open the reel for 0.3–0.5 s
    before the hook animation.
  - The two-character dialogue (questioner and explainer) for misconception topics. One
    shoot, two shirts, two backdrops. It needs the creator's agreement and a script
    written as a dialogue.
  - Cut-out objects in front of the speaker.
  - One giant keyword instead of a caption line, at the hook.
  - Real props in hand.
- **Leave:**
  - AI-generated images (a cat in clothes, an old man): our sourcing rule is real,
    licensed visuals.
  - Lip-synced or heavily styled colour washes on the skin.
