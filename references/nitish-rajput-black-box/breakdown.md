# Nitish Rajput: "How Black Boxes Survive Plane Crashes" (Hindi)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IGWdptXSUp0 (Nitish Rajput; 1.8M views when
studied). 73 s, 1080×1920. A Hindi science explainer.
Studied 2026-11-06 at 1080p: frames every 0.5 s to 9 s, every 2.5 s after, two full frames.
Pictures on the laptop only.

## Hook, 0–6 s (hook.png)

- **Frame 0 is the thumbnail, built as a split:** the top half has the presenter close up
  with a title card ("HOW BLACK BOX" in white caps over "SURVIVE CRASHES" in black caps on
  a yellow box); the bottom half is a 3D airliner flying at a mountain. The title states
  the question; the picture starts the story.
- Beats at 0.0 (split), about 2.0 (the plane hits, an explosion in the bottom half), 2.6
  (the explosion goes full screen), about 4.5 (black smoke), 5.5 (burning wreck with the
  orange black box lying in the fire). Five beats; longest still about 2.0 s, and the
  plane is moving the whole time.
- The presenter appears once more at 7.5 s for one line, then almost never again.

## Take / leave

- **Take:**
  - The split hook: presenter and title on top, the event already happening below, then
    the event takes the whole screen within about 2.5 s.
  - One continuous 3D world that the camera moves through, with numbers placed in the
    scene, not on cards. Our Remotion 3D can do this for sky, orbit and earth topics
    (Kullun fi Falak).
  - Red numbers with arrows on the object.
- **Leave:** a near-total absence of the presenter (our channel is the creator's voice and
  face); cinematic disaster imagery for its own sake.
