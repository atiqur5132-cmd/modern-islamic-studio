# Sound credits

Every sound in `public/sfx/` that we did not make ourselves. Sounds not listed here are
generated by `scripts/make_sfx.py` (no outside source). Rules are in `SOUND.md` §5.

All files below are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free to use, change and share,
no credit required. We credit the recordists anyway. The licence was read from each sound's
own Freesound page on 2026-11-18.

**Processing:** taken from Freesound's HQ preview (MP3), cut to one event, made mono,
48 kHz 16-bit WAV.
- Effects are trimmed and peak at −3 dBFS.
- Beds (`bed-`, `amb-`) are high-passed at 80 Hz, loudness-normalised (−20 LUFS; `bed-room`
  −30 LUFS) and faded 0.5 s in and 0.6 s out.
