Pichhle video pe aaya ye comment. Mazaak achha hai, bas Quran mein *udna* likha hi nahi.
Surah Nahl, verse pandrah. "An tamida bikum" (أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ).
"Tamida" aata hai "mayd" se. Matlab *dagmagana*, idhar-udhar jhool jaana.
Imam Tabari likhte hain, kashti lehron pe dagmagaati, toh Arab kehte "maadat".
Aur samandar mein jo *chakkar* aata hai, seasickness, usko bhi "mayd" kehte the.
Kabhi naav mein khade hue ho? Pair jamte nahi, sar ghoomne lagta hai.
Ab aakhri lafz, "bikum". Yaani *tumhare saath*.
Zameen tumhe lekar naav ki tarah na dagmagaaye.
Isiliye Surah Naml zameen ko "qarar" kehti hai (قَرَارًا). Tikne ki jagah. Ek *ghar*.
Aur Surah Naba pahadon ko kehti hai "awtad" (أَوْتَادًا). Tent ki *kheelein*.
Kheel na ho, toh tent hawa mein phadphadata hai.
Kuch mufassir ise poori zameen ka balance kehte hain, kuch rehne layak zameen. *Udna* kisi ne nahi kaha.
Aur jo kehte hain, "Pahad zalzale rokte hain"?
Ye bhi galat. 2015 ka Nepal wala zalzala, Himalaya ke beech hi aaya tha.
Aayat zalzalon se bachne ki guarantee nahi deti.
Comment mein dharti *ud* rahi thi. Aayat mein wo tumhe *tika* rahi hai.
Quran pe koi sawaal ho, toh comment karo. Agla jawab wahin se. Follow kar lo.
