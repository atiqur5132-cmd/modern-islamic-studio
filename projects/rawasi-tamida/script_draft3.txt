Pichhli reel par ye comment aaya.
Mazaak achha hai, bas ek chhoti si problem hai. Quran mein dharti ke *udne* ki baat hi nahi hai.
Surah Nahl, verse pandrah mein likha hai, "an tamida bikum" (أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ).
"Tamida" (تَمِيدَ) ka matlab hai hilna, dolna, *dagmagana*. Arab ye word lehron pe dolti kashti ke liye bolte the.
Aur "bikum" (بِكُمْ) matlab, *tumhare saath*.
Yaani zameen tumhe lekar na dole. Udna nahi, *dolna*.
Ab pahadon ka word dekho, "rawasi" (رَوَٰسِىَ).
Iska root wahi hai, jo Arabic mein kashti ka *langar* daalne ke liye aata hai.
Nuh alaihissalam ki kashti ke rukne ke liye bhi Quran yahi root use karta hai, "wa mursaha" (وَمُرْسَىٰهَآ).
Ab socho, anchor lehrein nahi rokta. Bas boat ko apni jagah tikaye rakhta hai.
Ye samjhane ke liye ek picture hai, Quran ka literal statement nahi.
Aur isi tarah, pahad *zalzale* nahi rokte.
2015 ka Nepal wala 7.8 ka zalzala, Himalaya ke beech hi aaya tha.
Toh jo kehte hain pahad earthquake-proof hain, wo verse mein apni baat daal rahe hain.
Aur jo kehte hain ye scientific error hai, wo bhool rahe hain ki tamida ka matlab specifically zalzala nahi hai.
Scholars ki raay alag hai, poori zameen ka balance ya rehne layak zameen. Udna kisi ne nahi kaha.
Aur haan, dharti ud nahi rahi thi bhai. Bas comment mein thodi zyada *flight* ho gayi.
