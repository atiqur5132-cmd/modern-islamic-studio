"Pahad gaad diye, taaki dharti *ud* na jaaye."
Pichhle video pe ye comment aaya. Sawaal achha hai, par Quran mein *udna* likha hi nahi.
Surah Nahl, verse pandrah. "An tamida bikum" (أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ).
"Tamida" (تَمِيدَ) bana hai "mayd" (مَيْد) se. Arab ye word *kashti* ke liye bolte the, jab wo lehron pe dagmagaati.
Imam Tabari likhte hain, seasickness ko bhi "mayd" (مَيْد) kehte the.
Kabhi naav mein khade hue ho? Pair jamte nahi, sar ghoomne lagta hai.
Aur aakhri word, "bikum" (بِكُمْ). Yaani *tumhare saath*.
Matlab, zameen tumhe lekar naav ki tarah na dagmagaaye.
Scholars ki raay alag hai, poori zameen ka balance ya rehne layak zameen. Par udna, kisi ne nahi kaha.
Toh kashti ko tikata kaun hai? *Langar*.
Aur pahadon ke liye Quran ka word, "rawasi" (رَوَٰسِىَ), usi root se hai jisse Arabic mein *langar daalna* bolte hain.
Lekin langar lehrein nahi rokta. Bas kashti ko itna tikata hai ki log us par reh sakein.
Isliye ye kehna bhi galat hai, ki pahad *zalzale* rok dete hain.
2015 ka Nepal wala zalzala, Himalaya ke beech hi aaya tha.
Comment mein dharti ud rahi thi. Quran mein wo ek *kashti* hai, aur pahad uske *langar*.
Quran pe koi sawaal ho, toh comment karo. Agla jawab wahin se. Follow kar lo.
