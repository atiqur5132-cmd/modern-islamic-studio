## The one idea

The verse says the earth would *sway with you*, like a boat makes you dizzy, not that it
would fly away; mountains are part of how Allah made the earth a steady home. The other
side's claim ("mountains stop earthquakes") is not in the verse either.

Picture: a boat rocking (mayd = what a ship does, and the seasickness it gives you), then a
tent held by its pegs (78:7).

Translations of 16:15 (quran.com, accessed 2026-11-12):
- Sahih International: "And He has cast into the earth firmly set mountains, lest it shift with you"
- Taqi Usmani: "And He placed mountains on the earth, lest it should shake with you"
- Mustafa Khattab: "so it does not shake with you"

None of the translations or tafsir pages read uses "fly" or anything like it.

## Tafsir

- **al-Tabari** on 16:15 (quran.com/ar/16:15/tafsirs/ar-tafsir-al-tabari, accessed 2026-11-12):
  - أن تميد بكم = "أن لا تميد بكم" (lest it sway with you); mountains set "لئلا يميد خلقه الذي على ظهرها".
  - "والميد: هو الاضطراب والتكفؤ": agitation and tilting.
  - Usage: "مادت السفينة تميد ميدا", the ship rolled; and "الميد الذي يعتري راكب البحر، وهو الدوار": the dizziness that comes over a sea traveller.
  - Mujahid: "أن تكفأ بكم", lest it tip over with you.
  - Reports about creation: Qays ibn 'Abbad via al-Hasan and Qatada, that the earth "began to shake" when created, the angels said it would hold no one, and by morning the mountains were in it; a report from Ali (chain through 'Ata' ibn al-Sa'ib, criticised for confusion late in life; to check with a scholar); al-Hasan: "كادت تميد". These are reports of the Companions/Successors, not marfu' hadith; **not used in the script.**
- **Ibn Kathir** (abridged English, quran.com):
  - 16:15: mountains "which make it stable and keep it from shaking".
  - 78:7: pegs "to hold it in place, make it stable and firm ... suitable for dwelling and not quake with those who are in it."
  - 27:61 qarar: "stable and stationary, so that it does not move or convulse".
- **Ma'ariful Quran** (Mufti Muhammad Shafi), 16:15: mayd = "to shake or swing or sway from side to side"; he reads it as the earth as a whole being balanced by the weight of mountains, and says the Quran "neither affirms nor rejects" the earth's motion.

**Scholarly difference:** some read the swaying as the earth as a whole (Ma'ariful Quran, the creation reports in Tabari); others put the weight on the human side, a firm, liveable ground "with you" (Ibn Kathir's "suitable for dwelling"). Nobody read it as the earth flying off.

## Science

- Nepal earthquake, 25 April 2015, Mw 7.8–7.9, about 9,000 dead; caused by thrusting on the boundary "where the Indian plate underthrusts the Eurasian plate" (Wikipedia, "April 2015 Nepal earthquake", citing USGS; accessed 2026-11-12). USGS pages were not reachable by the fetcher; check the USGS event page before the figure goes on screen.
- The Himalaya rose from the India–Eurasia collision, and that movement "makes this region seismically active" (Wikipedia, "Himalayas").
- Mountains have roots: crust is thickest under ranges, as much as 80 km in collision zones vs about 40 km average (Wikipedia, "Isostasy"; "Continental crust"); Airy and Pratt, 1855. **Parked**, not in the script: it serves a different idea (pegs and roots), and the pegs image was known to pre-Islamic Arabs, per critics; using it invites a miracle claim we cannot carry in 50 s.
