Quranic text
- 88:17, 88:18, 88:19, 88:20, 79:30 (also fetched: 71:19, 15:19, 39:5): api.quran.com v4
  (text_uthmani), cross-checked with api.alquran.cloud (quran-uthmani) by

Scholar cards (English renderings of the Arabic in research.md)
- Line 11 ("Kuch purane scholars bhi ise flat hi samajh rahe the") has no card on screen, by
  the creator's choice. Its source: Tafsir al-Jalalayn (al-Mahalli) and Ibn Atiyya on 88:20
  (research.md).
- Line 12: Fakhr al-Din al-Razi, Mafatih al-Ghayb on 88:20, "when a ball is very large,
  every piece of it is like a flat surface".
