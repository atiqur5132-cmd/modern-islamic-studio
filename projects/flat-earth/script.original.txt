Zameen *flat* hai, aur ye Quran mein likha hai.
Jawab milta hai, "Nahi bhai, Quran ne toh zameen ko *anda* bola hai."
Maine dono check kiye. Sach bataun? Dono hi galat nikle.
Pehle flat wala. Surah Ghashiyah ke verse 20 mein word hai, "sutihat" (سُطِحَتْ).
Matlab, *bichha dena*. Jaise chadar bichhate hain.
Par baat kis cheez ki ho rahi hai? Thoda peeche padho.
Quran poochta hai, ye log oont ko nahi dekhte? Aasmaan ko, pahadon ko?
Aur zameen ko, ki wo kaise *bichhayi* gayi?
Socho, registan mein ek aadmi oont pe baitha hai. Yahi chaar cheezein toh uske saamne hain.
Baat uske pairon ke neeche ki zameen ki hai. Planet ki shape ki nahi.
Lekin main honest rahunga. Kuch purane scholars ne bhi ise flat hi samjha tha, jaise Tafsir Jalalayn.
Par Imam Razi ne isi verse pe likha, ki bahut badi ball ka har hissa *flat* hi dikhta hai.
Tum abhi jahan khade ho, zameen flat lag rahi hai na? Phir bhi gol hai.
Ab ande wala claim. Surah Nazi'at ke verse 30 mein word hai "dahaha" (دَحَىٰهَآ).
Kehte hain, matlab shutur-murg ka anda. Par dictionary mein iska matlab bhi wahi hai, *phailana*.
Shutur-murg se rishta hai, par ande ka nahi, uske *nest* ka.
Wo ret phaila ke nest banata hai, aur usi ko "udhiyy" (أُدْحِيّ) kehte hain.
Toh ek ne verse se flat zameen nikaal li. Doosre ne anda.
Aur verse bas itna keh raha tha, dekho, ye zameen tumhare liye kaise *bichhayi* gayi.
