Caption (with hook 1):

The Quran says the Earth is flat? Or shaped like an egg? Both viral claims fall apart. 👇

🌍 Claim 1: "Surah Ghashiyah 88:20 says the Earth is flat."
The word is sutihat: spread out, laid out. Read the verses before it (88:17–20): "Then do they not look at the camels - how they are created? And at the sky - how it is raised? And at the mountains - how they are erected? And at the earth - how it is spread out?" Picture a man riding through the desert: that is everything in front of him. Qurtubi, Ibn Kathir and Razi read it as the ground under his feet, not the shape of the planet.
Some classical scholars did read it as flat. But Imam Razi wrote on this same verse that every part of a very large ball looks flat. Where you're standing right now looks flat too.

🥚 Claim 2: "Surah Naziat 79:30 says the Earth is egg-shaped."
The word is dahaha, and it also means spread out. The ostrich link is real, but it's the nest, not the egg: the ostrich spreads the sand to make its nest, and Arabic calls that nest udhiyy. "Dahaha = ostrich egg" isn't in the classical tafsirs we checked.

So one side read "flat" into the verse, the other read "egg". The verse simply asks you to look at the earth, and how it was spread out.

## Pinned first comment (both platforms)

Some classical scholars did read 88:20 as describing a flat earth. Others, like Imam Razi, said a very large sphere looks flat from any point on it. The words themselves, sutihat (88:20) and dahaha (79:30), both mean "spread out". Sources are in the caption.
