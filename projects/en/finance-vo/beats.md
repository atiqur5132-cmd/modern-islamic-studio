## Look (numbers before building)

- Ground `#15191d` (slate blackboard, not pure black), second ground `#1d2228` for chalk dust smudges.
- Chalk `#ece8df` (warm white), dim chalk `#8d939b` for secondary marks.
- One accent `#ef7f6c` coral. One meaning all video: **what it really costs you** (the lost
  discount, the tax, the fee, the locked limit, the 40% of the paycheck). Never used for
  decoration or for the bank/brand.
- Hand: Caveat 600 for every label. Numbers that must be read big: Caveat 700.
  Serif Newsreader italic only for the last line ("your future freedom").
- Lines: 5 px chalk with a light displacement (static, no boil) and a dusty texture mask.
- Arrivals: strokes draw over 12–30 frames, eased out; text writes left to right at
  ~14 characters per 10 frames. Camera moves 24–40 frames, ease in-out, never a whip.
- Margins ≥ 120 px. Fine grain + soft vignette.
- Claim credits (small, dim chalk, bottom-left) where a number is a claim:
  "India · RBI circular 2013" on the 0% line, "Illustration" on the worked example.
