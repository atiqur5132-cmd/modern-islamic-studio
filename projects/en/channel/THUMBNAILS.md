# The Rich Rules — thumbnail playbook (v2, 2026-11-21)

The standard for every Rich Rules thumbnail from "Deadbeats" onward. Built on the CLICK method
(social-media-skills `thumbnail-design`, read in full 2026-11-21; its numbers are unsourced rules
of thumb, so we judge by our own tests and YouTube's Test & Compare). Code:
`src/compositions/RewardsThumbnail.tsx` (3D scene + type), `src/long/rewards/thumbTextures.ts`.

## The look (fixed for the channel)

- **Hero:** one photoreal 3D object rendered in three.js (real light, real shadow), never flat
  clip art. Money objects are gold metal (the gold card is the channel's recurring hero).
- **Ground:** spotlit deep teal, `#2a625a` centre → `#0c2321` edges. Not near-black: it must keep an
  edge on YouTube's dark UI (`#0f0f0f`).
- **Light:** warm key from the upper left, teal rim from behind, soft floor shadow.
- **Type:** **Anton** (creator's pick, 2026-11-21), capitals, 2–4 words, left 40% of the frame,
  white with one key word in yellow `#ffd23f`. Soft drop shadow, no thick outline.
- **Accent red** `#a50f08` only for the cost or verdict, and only on an object (a stamp, a total).
- **Never:** a face or the creator's photo, the channel name or logo, YouTube red/white as a
  ground, more than one focal idea, small text that matters.

Thumbnails are generated by the creator in an image AI from prompts Claude writes:
`THUMBNAIL_PROMPTS.md` holds the five fixed signatures, the brand style block and the template.

## Making one (every video)

1. **Promise first:** one sentence — "This video shows [viewer] why [outcome] by revealing
   [mechanism]."
2. **Show vs tell:** the thumbnail shows the hook, the title tells the context; they never repeat
   each other's words.
3. **Three concepts**, each a different reason to click (for example anger, doubt, fear of cost).
   One hero object each, plus at most one tension prop.
4. **Truthful:** the picture must be a promise the video keeps (Test & Compare scores watch-time
   share, so bait loses).
5. **Render** stills at 1280×720, then the finals with `--scale=2` (2560×1440, PNG).

## Tests (all must pass before showing the creator)

| Test | How | Pass |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone feed | scale to 168×94 | words and the key prop readable |
| Desktop feed | scale to 320×180 on `#0f0f0f` and `#ffffff` | edge visible in both modes |
| Black and white | desaturate | every element distinct (text never on a same-brightness object) |
| Safe zone | look at bottom-right 12% and bottom 15% | nothing important under the duration badge |
| Clutter | squint / 1-second look | one focal point |

The feed sheet is built with one ffmpeg filter (see `projects/en/card-rewards/thumb_feed_test.png`).
