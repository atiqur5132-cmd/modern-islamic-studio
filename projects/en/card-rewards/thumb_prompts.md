# Thumbnail prompts for another image AI — "Deadbeats"

For an image model (Nano Banana / Gemini, ChatGPT image, Midjourney, Ideogram). Each follows
`projects/en/channel/THUMBNAILS.md`. Text-in-image works best in ChatGPT image or Ideogram; with
Midjourney, use the "no text" version and add the words in Canva (Anton, white + yellow).

## Check every result before using it

- Spelling: "DEADBEAT", "MONEY?", "PAYS?", "YOU" exactly; image AIs often misspell. Redo or fix in Canva.
- Shrink it to 168×94 px: the words and the main object must still read.
- Turn it black and white: the yellow word must not melt into the gold card.
- No stray text, fake logos or card brand marks on the card.
- Bottom-right corner empty (the duration badge sits there).
