📚 Sources and notes
• Interest and fees: US credit card issuers charged over $130 billion in interest and fees in 2022 (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, The Consumer Credit Card Market report, 2023).
• Interchange fees: typically about 2–3% of the purchase in the US for rewards cards; rates vary by card network, card type and merchant.
• MIT experiment: Prelec & Simester (2001), "Always Leave Home Without It: A Further Investigation of the Credit-Card Effect on Willingness to Pay", Marketing Letters. People paying by card bid far more for the same sports tickets.
• "12–18% more with plastic" is a widely repeated estimate; studies differ on the size of the effect.
• The $2,000 example is an illustration: 24% APR, minimum payment = 1% of the balance + that month's interest, $25 floor. It takes about 12 years and roughly $2,800 in interest to clear. Your card's minimum-payment formula may differ.
• "Deadbeat" and "revolver" are industry slang, not official terms.

This video is for education only and is not financial advice. Check your own card's terms, rates and fees.
