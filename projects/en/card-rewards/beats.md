## Fact check (2026-11-21)

- $2,000 at 24% with minimum payments only: about 11–12 years and about $2,750–2,900 in interest
  (computed both at 1% + interest and at 3% of balance, $25 floor). "Well over $1,000" holds.
- $130B a year in interest and fees: CFPB figure for 2022. Holds.
- Interchange 2–3%, APR 22–36%, breakage and devaluation, "deadbeat" and "revolver" as industry
  slang: all hold.
- MIT experiment: Prelec & Simester (2001), auction for sports tickets; card payers bid far more.
- "12–18% more with plastic": widely repeated, but there is no solid primary study. The voice is already recorded,
  so the line stays, but it gets no big number on screen.
