# Algorithm is a man's name: research

Series B, Forgotten History. TOPICS.md #3. Target length 30–40 s (creator's request,
2026-11-12), so about 115 words at 3.3 words a second.

**The one idea (for a 15-year-old):** the word "algorithm" is the Latin spelling of a
Baghdad scholar's name, al-Khwarizmi, and his name itself means "the man from Khwarazm".

**The picture:** the name changing letter by letter on screen:
al-Khwarizmi → Algoritmi → algorism → algorithm, with the phone feed behind it.

## What the reel does NOT say

- Not "al-Khwarizmi invented the algorithm" or "computers come from Islam".
- Not "he was born in Uzbekistan" (birthplace disputed, row 7).
- Not "he invented zero" or "Arabic numerals". MacTutor says his use of zero as a
  placeholder "was probably" the first; that is a separate, contested claim (and TOPICS #34).
- No "father of algebra" line. Assessments of his originality differ sharply (Toomer
  "mediocre", Rashed "impossible to overstress", S1).
