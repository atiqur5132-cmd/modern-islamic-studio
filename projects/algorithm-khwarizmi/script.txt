Ye reel tum tak ek *algorithm* laaya hai.
Aur ye word, asal mein ek aadmi ka *naam* hai.
Baarah sau saal pehle, Baghdad mein ek scholar the, Muhammad ibn Musa *al-Khwarizmi*.
Unhone ek kitaab likhi, ki Hindustan ke numbers se hisaab kaise lagaya jaata hai.
Teen sau saal baad, wo kitaab Latin mein *Europe* pahunchi.
Aur uski pehli line thi, "Dixit Algoritmi". Yaani, al-Khwarizmi ne kaha.
Centuries tak log ye naam bolte rahe, aur bolte-bolte wo ban gaya *algorithm*.
Haan, hisaab ke tareeqe unse pehle bhi the. Euclid ka tareeqa toh hazaar saal purana tha.
Bas *naam* unka chal pada.
Agli baar jab feed scroll karoge, yaad rakhna.
Tum Baghdad ke ek scholar ka *naam* le rahe ho.
