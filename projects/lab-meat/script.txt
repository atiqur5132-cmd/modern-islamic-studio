[CHAPTER: Hook]
Is chicken ko kisi ne *zabah* nahi kiya.
Yeh gosht kabhi kisi farm mein nahi pala.
Yeh ek *machine* ke andar bada hua hai.
Aur Singapore mein muftiyon ki ek committee ne kaha hai ki yeh *halal* ho sakta hai.
[SHOW: MUIS, Fatwa on cultivated meat, 3 Feb 2024]
Lekin unhone saath mein kuch shartein bhi rakhi.
Aur unmein se ek shart aisi hai jo aaj ki poori industry ke liye *sabse mushkil* hai.
Yeh gosht banta kaise hai, Islam ise kis nazar se dekhta hai, aur scholars kahan ek baat par hain, kahan alag, sab ek ek karke.
Aur end tak tum khud samajh jaoge ki aaj supermarket mein ise dekho toh kya poochna hai.

[CHAPTER: Ek burger, dhai lakh euro]
Kahani shuru hoti hai 2013 mein, London mein.
Ek press conference mein ek burger pakaya gaya.
Dikhne mein normal burger, lekin uska gosht kisi janwar ko kaat kar nahi aaya tha.
Use Netherlands ke ek scientist, Mark Post, ki team ne lab mein ugaya tha.
Aur us ek burger par kharcha aaya, lagbhag *dhai lakh euro*.
[SHOW: NPR, 5 Aug 2013]
Saat saal baad, December 2020 mein, Singapore duniya ka *pehla* desh bana jisne aise gosht ko bechne ki permission di.
Phir America, Israel, Australia ne bhi kuch products ko approve kiya.
Toh ab yeh science fiction nahi hai. Yeh ek real product hai.

[CHAPTER: Gosht banta kaise hai]
Ise samajhne ke liye ek seedhi si cheez yaad rakho.
Gosht asal mein *cells* se bana hota hai. Chhote chhote zinda tukde.
Janwar ke andar yeh cells khaana aur oxygen paakar badhte rehte hain.
Scientists ne socha, agar cells ko bahar bhi wahi khaana aur wahi garmi mil jaaye, toh woh bahar bhi badhenge.
Toh pehle janwar se cells ka ek bahut chhota sa sample liya jaata hai.
Phir un cells ko ek special liquid mein daala jaata hai, jise *growth medium* kehte hain.
Is liquid mein sugar, protein, vitamins, sab kuch hota hai jo cells ko chahiye.
Yeh sab ek bade steel ke tank mein hota hai, jise *bioreactor* kehte hain.
Tank ke andar temperature janwar ke jism jaisa rakha jaata hai, aur oxygen di jaati hai.
Do se aath hafte mein woh thode se cells *billions of cells* ban jaate hain.
Phir unhe plant se bane ek dhaanche par jamaya jaata hai, taaki gosht jaisa texture aaye.
Bas, yahi hai lab-grown meat. Isse *cultivated meat* bhi kehte hain.
Lekin ek problem hai. Yeh abhi bhi bahut mehenga hai.
Isliye May 2024 mein Singapore ki ek dukaan par jo pehla pack bika, usmein asli lab wala chicken sirf *3 percent* tha.
Baaki 97 percent plants se bana tha.
[SHOW: Food Navigator, 21 May 2024]

[CHAPTER: Halal ke teen sawaal]
Ab aate hain asli baat par.
Bahut log sochte hain ki halal ka matlab bas itna hai ki suar ka gosht na ho.
Lekin Islamic law kisi bhi gosht se *teen* cheezein poochta hai.
Pehli, yeh kis janwar ka hai.
Doosri, ise kaise liya gaya.
Teesri, ise kis cheez se pala gaya.
Normal gosht mein teeno ka jawab seedha hota hai.
Halal janwar, sahi tareeke se zabah, aur halal chaara.
Lekin lab wale gosht mein teeno sawaal naye ho jaate hain.
Aur scholars ke beech saari bahas inhi teen sawaalon par hai.

[CHAPTER: Pehla sawaal: kis janwar ka cell]
Pehla sawaal sabse aasaan hai.
Lagbhag har scholar aur har fatwa body yahan ek baat par hai.
Cell kisi aise janwar ka hona chahiye jo Muslims ke liye khaana *halal* hai.
Murgi, bakra, gaay, haan.
Suar, nahi.
Kuch log yeh bhi kehte hain ki lab mein cell itna badal jaata hai ki woh nayi cheez ban jaata hai.
Fiqh mein ise *istihala* kehte hain, yaani poori tarah badal jaana.
Lekin yeh daleel bahut vivadit hai, aur badi fatwa bodies ne ise nahi apnaya.
Singapore ki fatwa ki toh pehli shart hi yahi hai ki cell halal janwar ka ho.

[CHAPTER: Doosra sawaal: zinda janwar se liya gaya cell]
Ab doosra sawaal, aur yahin se bahas shuru hoti hai.
Cells ka sample aksar *zinda* janwar se liya jaata hai.
Ek chhoti si sui, thoda sa tissue, aur janwar aaraam se ghoomta rehta hai.
Sunne mein yeh janwar ke liye achhi baat lagti hai.
Lekin ek hadith hai jo yahan bahut important ho jaati hai.
Jab Nabi sallallahu alaihi wasallam Madinah aaye, toh wahan log zinda oonton ka kuhaan aur zinda bhedon ki dum ka gosht kaat lete the.
Janwar zinda rehta tha, aur gosht kha liya jaata tha.
Aap ne farmaya, zinda janwar se jo hissa kaata jaaye, woh *maytah* hai.
Yaani murdaar, jo khaana mana hai.
[SHOW: Jami at-Tirmidhi 1480, Hasan]
Toh kuch scholars kehte hain ki zinda janwar se liya gaya cell bhi wahi hai.
Ek kaata hua hissa.
Isliye unke hisaab se cell sirf aise janwar se liya jaaye jo sahi tareeke se *zabah* hua ho.
Malaysia ki official fatwa body, JAKIM, ki fatwa ke baare mein reports yahi shart batati hain.
American Fiqh Academy ne bhi kaha ki zinda janwar se liye gaye cells se bana gosht halal nahi.
Lekin doosri taraf bhi scholars hain.
Woh kehte hain ki hadith mein baat gosht ke ek tukde ki hai, jo kaat kar seedha kha liya jaata tha.
Ek microscopic cell, jo hafton tak tank mein badhta hai, woh alag cheez hai.
International Islamic Fiqh Academy mein fatwa department ke director rahe Dr Abdul Qahir Qamar ne iski zindagi ko paudhon jaisa kaha.
Unki misaal thi, dahi aur achaar jaisi zindagi.
Aur Singapore ki fatwa ne shart rakhi ki janwar halal ho. Zabah ki shart unke fatwa mein likhi nahi hai.
Toh yahan sach yeh hai ki scholars *ek raay* par nahi hain.
Aur dono taraf ke paas daleel hai.

[CHAPTER: Teesra sawaal: cells ko kya khilaya]
Ab teesra sawaal, aur yahi woh shart hai jo industry ke liye sabse mushkil hai.
Yaad hai woh growth medium, woh liquid jismein cells badhte hain.
Saalon tak is liquid ka ek important hissa tha *fetal bovine serum*.
Yeh gaay ke pet mein pal rahe bachhe ke *khoon* se nikalta hai.
Aur Quran khoon ko khaane ke liye haram kehta hai.
[SHOW: Ayah 5:3]
Isliye jo bhi scholar lab wale gosht ko halal ho sakta hai kehte hain, woh saath mein yeh bhi kehte hain.
Agar cells ko khoon wala serum diya gaya, toh gosht halal nahi.
Achhi baat yeh hai ki science bhi ab isi taraf ja rahi hai.
Serum mehenga hai, aur janwar se aata hai, toh kai companies ab bina serum ke medium bana rahi hain.
Lekin har ingredient halal ho, iski guarantee chahiye.
Growth factors, enzymes, har cheez ka source.
Aur yahi kaam halal certification ka hota hai.

[CHAPTER: Fatwas ne kya kaha]
Toh ab tak kis ne kya kaha, ek baar saaf saaf dekh lete hain.
February 2024 mein Singapore ki fatwa committee ne kaha, shartein poori hon toh halal.
Teen shartein. Halal janwar ka cell, har ingredient halal, aur product saaf aur safe.
Unki daleel thi, insaan ki jaan ki hifazat, mahaul ki hifazat, aur ek purana usool.
Jo cheez faydemand hai, woh jaayaz hai, jab tak uske khilaf daleel na mil jaaye.
Korea ki Muslim Federation ne bhi yahi position li.
Malaysia, jo khud ek Muslim-majority desh hai, uski JAKIM ne bhi kaha ki halal ho sakta hai, lekin reports ke mutabik zabah wali shart ke saath, aur bina khoon wale medium ke.
Aur May 2025 mein Qatar ke Doha mein International Islamic Fiqh Academy baithi.
Yeh OIC ki fiqh academy hai, jiski baat bahut se Muslim desh maante hain.
Wahan cultivated meat par research papers padhe gaye.
News reports ne likha ki Academy ne shartein ke saath ijazat di.
[SHOW: IIFA Resolution 265, Doha 2025: cells from a lawful animal, alive or slaughtered where required; no blood or pig gelatin; supervised; disclosed; not a replacement]

Ab woh baat jo zyada tar log miss kar dete hain.
Fatwa ek *usool* batati hai. Certificate ek *product* ko check karta hai.
Singapore ki fatwa ne bhi khud kaha ki certificate ke liye aur sakht rules chahiye honge.
Aur jab yeh fatwa aayi, tab ek bhi lab-grown product un rules par poora nahi utarta tha.

Yaani fatwa ne darwaza khola hai.
[SHOW: UPSIDE Foods / New Food, 17 Nov 2026: first halal certificate for a cultivated meat (UPSIDE Chicken, USA, Circle H), per the company]

Italy ne 2023 mein, aur America ke Florida aur Alabama ne 2024 mein, is gosht ki bikri par hi *ban* laga diya.
Wahan wajah religion nahi thi, zyada tar farmers aur apni food culture ki fikr thi.
