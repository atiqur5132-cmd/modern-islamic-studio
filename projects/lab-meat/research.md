## The one idea

Islamic law did not answer "new technology, so haram" or "no pig, so halal". It asks three questions of any
meat: which animal the cell came from, how it was taken, and what it was fed. Every scholar's answer on lab-grown
meat comes down to those three, and today's products do not yet clear all of them.

## What the creator should know first

- **IIFA (corrected 2026-11-24).** The Academy published a resolution on cultivated meat itself: No. 265 (10/26),
  Doha session 4–8 May 2025, posted 20 Nov 2025 (https://iifa-aifi.org/en/56085.html). It permits it if (1) the
  cells come from an animal lawful to eat *if it is alive*, or from one slaughtered Islamically where slaughter is
  required; (2) no prohibited medium such as flowing blood, no prohibited substance such as pig gelatin; (3) the
  whole process is supervised by a credible specialised authority; companies must disclose; safe for health; and
  it should not replace conventional meat. Some press reports said "slaughtered animals only": the text says
  otherwise. (No. 266, read first by mistake, is the separate ruling on GM animal food.) The script was fixed.
- **"Slaughtered or not" is where bodies differ.** MUIS (Singapore) and IIFA (Res. 265) accept a living halal
  animal; JAKIM (Malaysia, per press), the American Fiqh Academy and Hamdan et al. ask for a slaughtered one.
- **No ruling of ours.** The video explains positions; it never says "so it is halal". Fiqh lines need a
  qualified scholar's glance before upload (creator's call).
