Chiti ke paas zabaan nahi hoti. Lekin khatra aaye, toh wo bhi *chillati* hai.
Bas uski cheekh awaaz nahi, ek *khushboo* hoti hai.
Khatra mehsoos hote hi, wo hawa mein ek chemical chhod deti hai.
Scientist E. O. Wilson ne 1958 mein ise naapa tha. Paas wali chitiyan, chaar se nau second mein alert.
Phir wo bhi wahi khushboo chhodti hain. Aur dekhte hi dekhte, poora bil jaag jaata hai.
Ab yahi manzar Quran mein dekho. Surah Naml mein.
Sulaiman alaihissalam ki fauj ek waadi se guzar rahi thi. Chitiyon ki waadi.
Tabhi ek chiti boli,
"Ae chitiyo, apne gharon mein chali jao.
Kahin Sulaiman aur unka lashkar tumhe kuchal na de... aur unhe *pata bhi na chale*."
Khatra dekha, sabko khabar kar di. Bilkul asli chitiyon ki tarah.
Aur Allah ke diye ilm se, Sulaiman ne uski baat samajh li.
Lekin is kahani ka sabse khoobsurat hissa abhi baaki hai.
Uski aakhri baat phir se suno. "Unhe pata bhi na chale."
Poori qaum ki jaan khatre mein thi. Phir bhi usne fauj ko *zaalim* nahi kaha.
Bas itna kaha, wo jaan boojh kar aisa nahi karenge.
Imam Qurtubi likhte hain, kuch ulama ke mutabiq Sulaiman isi baat par muskuraye the.
Ek chhoti si chiti, aur itna bada *dil*.
Aur hum? Kisi ka reply thoda late aa jaaye, toh dimaag mein poori kahani bana lete hain.
Science ne chiti ki *cheekh* naapi. Quran ne uska *dil* dikhaya.
Aisi aayatein aur samajhni hain? Toh follow kar lo.
