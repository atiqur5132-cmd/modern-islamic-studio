Sharaab mein *fayda* hai. Yeh Quran kehta hai.
Haan, sahi suna tumne. Quran ki Surah Baqarah, verse 219.
"Inmein badi buraai hai, aur logon ke liye kuch *fayde* bhi hain."
Aur agli hi line mein: "Lekin inki buraai, inke fayde se *badi* hai."
Toh sawaal yeh banta hai ki jab fayda maan liya, toh phir mana kyun kiya.
Iske liye ek tarazu imagine karo.
Ek taraf woh fayde rakho, jo purane Islamic scholars ne khud likhe hain.
Thodi khushi, thoda maza, aur bechne walon ka *profit*.
Ab doosri taraf ka hisaab dekho, jo aaj World Health Organisation deta hai.
Duniya mein har saal lagbhag *26 lakh* log sirf sharaab ki wajah se mar jaate hain.
Yaani har roz lagbhag *7 hazaar* log.
Lekin dhyaan do, Quran ne mana karte waqt health ki baat ki hi nahi.
Usne kaha, isse logon ke beech *dushmani* aur nafrat badhti hai.
Aur insaan Allah ko, namaz ko bhool jaata hai.
Ek Muslim ke liye toh yeh Allah ka hukm hai, data ho ya na ho.
But data dekh ke koi bhi samajh jayega ki iske peeche logic kya hai.
Ab science ki baat karte hain.
2022 mein duniya ke bade medical journal, The Lancet, mein ek *badi* study aayi.
Study ke hisaab se, 40 saal se upar ke kuch logon ko bahut thodi sharaab se *shayad* thoda fayda ho sakta hai.
Lekin 15 se 39 saal ke logon ke liye fayda *zero* hai. Sirf risk hai.
Yaani wahi tarazu, jo Quran ne rakha tha. Fayda hai, par nuksaan *bada* hai.
Pehle fayda gina, phir nuksaan gina, phir faisla kiya.
*Not a blind order. A balance sheet.*
Muslims ke baare mein koi bhi sawaal ho, toh comment karo, aur follow karo.
