# Oldest university: Nalanda vs al-Qarawiyyin — research

Series: A, Viral Claim Check (two-sided). Checked 2026-11-17.

**One idea:** "Sabse purani university" has no single answer. It depends on what you count: the first big residential centre of learning (Nalanda), the oldest one still running (al-Qarawiyyin), or the oldest "university" in the European degree-giving sense (Bologna).

Source status: the points below come from search results (2026-11-17). Before the final render, open the Guinness page, the Dharmasvamin biography (Roerich translation) and the Encyclopaedia of Islam entry "al-Karawiyyin" directly.

In 1235 the Tibetan monk Dharmasvamin found only two of its viharas in use and fewer than a hundred monks under the aged abbot Rahula Shribhadra, with raids still going on. ## Parked (true, but not this reel's idea)

- A new Nalanda University was set up by the Nalanda University Act 2010. It opened in 2014 with 14 students; its Rajgir campus, about 12 km from the ruins, was inaugurated 19 June 2024. It is a new institution, not a continuation.
- al-Azhar, Cairo (970 CE), is the other "oldest still running" contender.
- Popular "library burned for three months" story: no contemporary source. Do not use it.

## Numbers

- Nalanda's span: 1200 − 427 ≈ 773 years, so "about 800" is loose; the script avoids the figure.
- al-Qarawiyyin's age: 2026 − 859 = 1,167 years.
- Hijri: 245 AH starts in March 859 CE (checked against the standard conversion: 245 × 0.970229 + 621.57 ≈ 859.3).
