Duniya ki sabse purani university kaunsi hai?
Ye sawaal poocho, to do naam aate hain. *Nalanda*... aur *al-Qarawiyyin*.
Aur log aapas mein ladte rehte hain ki sahi kaun hai.
Mazedaar baat ye hai ki dono sahi hain. Bas dono alag sawaal ka jawab hain.
Pehle Nalanda chalte hain. Bihar, 5th century, Gupta rajaon ka zamaana.
Socho, ek aisi jagah jahan hazaaron log padhte bhi the, aur wahin rehte bhi the.
China aur Korea se log mahino ka safar karke yahan padhne aate the.
Phir 1200 ke aas-paas Turk hamle hue, aur Nalanda ko bahut nuksaan pahuncha.
Log kehte hain Bakhtiyar Khalji ne ise jalaya. Lekin us zamaane ki history book mein Nalanda ka naam tak nahi aata.
Haan, 1235 mein ek Tibetan monk wahan pahuncha, to sirf do building mein padhai bachi thi.
Aur phir dheere-dheere wahan *sannata* chha gaya.
Ab Morocco chalte hain. Fez shehar, saal 859.
Wahan ek masjid banti hai, al-Qarawiyyin.
Kehte hain Fatima al-Fihri naam ki ek aurat ne apni virasat ka paisa isme laga diya.
Masjid mein padhai shuru hui... aur woh aaj tak *ruki nahi*.
Isliye Guinness ise duniya ka sabse purana *chalta hua* educational institution maanta hai.
Aur ek twist bhi hai. Agar university ka matlab degree dene wala system hai, to record Italy ke Bologna ke paas hai, 1088 se.
To jawab is par depend karta hai ki tum pooch kya rahe ho.
Ilm ka darwaza sabse pehle kisne khola? *Nalanda*.
Aur kaunsa darwaza aaj tak khula hai? *al-Qarawiyyin*.
