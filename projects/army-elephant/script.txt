Haathiyon wali ek army, aur uska target tha *Kaaba*.
Ye kahani tumne suni hogi. Par ye shayad nahi suna.
Is army ke maalik ne apni jangein *patthar par* likhwayi thi.
Naam tha Abraha. 6th century mein Yemen ka ruler, Ethiopia ke Aksum kingdom ki taraf se.
Uska ek patthar aaj bhi maujood hai, saal 552 ka.
Likha hai ki uski fauj ne central Arabia ki tribes par chadhai ki, jang jeeti, aur loyalty ki kasam li.
Toh kya yahi wo army thi jo Makkah aayi?
Lekin main honest rahunga.
Is patthar mein na Makkah ka naam hai, na Kaaba ka, na haathiyon ka.
Kuch historians ise wahi campaign maante hain, aur kuch nahi. Traditionally ye waqia lagbhag 570 ka maana jaata hai.
Ab dekho Quran kya kehta hai. Sirf *paanch* aayatein.
"Kya tumne nahi dekha, tumhare Rab ne haathi walon ke saath kya kiya?"
"Kya unki chaal ko bekaar nahi kar diya?"
"Aur un par parindon ke jhund ke jhund bheje," ababil (أَبَابِيلَ).
"Jo un par pakki mitti ke patthar phenkte the," sijjil (سِجِّيلٍ).
"Aur unhe khaaye hue *bhoose* jaisa kar diya."
Na church ka zikr, na oonton ka, na haathi ke naam ka. Wo sab baad ki seerat aur tafsir se aata hai.
Jaise Abdul Muttalib ka mashhoor jawab: "Main oonton ka maalik hoon. Is Ghar ka bhi ek Maalik hai, wo khud iski hifazat karega."
Aur wo parinde kya the? Is par scholars ek nahi hain.
Maududi kehte hain, sach mein parinde the, pattharon ke saath. Muhammad Asad kehte hain, shayad ek achanak bimari phaili thi.
Par ek baat par sab tafsir ek hain. Haathiyon wali wo army, Kaaba ko *gira nahi saki*.
Ek baadshah ne apni jeet patthar par likhwayi.
Aur uski haar, paanch aayaton mein, aaj bhi namazon mein padhi jaati hai.
Aisi aur chhupi history ke liye follow kar lo.
