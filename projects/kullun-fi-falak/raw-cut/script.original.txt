Quran mein ek line hai... jiske letters aap *ulta* padho, toh bhi same rehte hain.
Aur mazedaar baat ye hai ki wo line hai hi ghoomne ke baare mein.
Surah Al-Anbiya, ayat 33.
Isme raat, din, Sun aur Moon ki baat hoti hai...
aur phir aata hai: "Kullun fi falakin yasbahun."
Matlab, sab apne-apne falak mein tair rahe hain.
Ab ek kaam karo. Pehle teen words lo, aur saare vowel marks hata do.
Sirf jo letters likhe hue hain, wo dekho.
Kaaf. Laam. Fa.
Ya. Fa. Laam. Kaaf.
Ab ulti side se padho. *Same.*
Haan, ek cheez clear kar doon. Bolne mein ye ulta same nahi lagega.
Ye likhawat ka palindrome hai, awaaz ka nahi.
Ab falak ka matlab samjho. Koi bhi gol cheez, gol raasta.
Aur Ibn Abbas se riwayat hai ki unhone ise charkhe ki ghoomti hui chakri se samjhaya tha.
Aur Sun? Wo bhi ek jagah ruka hua nahi hai.
Lagbhag *230 kilometre per second* ki speed se poori galaxy ka chakkar laga raha hai.
Aur ek chakkar poora hone mein? Takreeban *23 crore saal* lagte hain.
Lekin main honest rahunga. Arabic mein aur bhi palindromes hain.
Aur purane zamaane ke astronomers bhi maante the ki Sun ghoomta hai.
Toh main ye nahi keh raha ki ye koi proof hai.
Bas ek baat socho. Jis line ka matlab hai "sab apne circle mein ghoom rahe hain"...
uske letters bhi ghoom ke wahin wapas aa jaate hain, jahan se shuru hue the.
Kuch lines sirf baat nahi karti.
Wo khud wahi ban jaati hain... jiske baare mein wo baat kar rahi hain.
