Kullun fi falakin yasbahun: sources and credits
================================================

Quranic text
- Surah Al-Anbiya 21:33, text_uthmani from api.quran.com v4, cross-checked with
  api.alquran.cloud (quran-uthmani) by scripts/fetch_ayah.py on 2026-11-03.
  alquran.cloud also prints the small iqlab meem (U+06ED, U+06E2); these tajweed-only
  marks are ignored in the comparison, every letter and vowel matched.
- The same phrase occurs in Surah Ya-Sin 36:40.
- G4 letters: the first three words of the fetched text with every haraka removed.
  The fetched Uthmani text writes the ya of "fi" without dots (U+0649); by the
  creator's decision the bare-letter graphic shows it as the dotted ya (U+064A).
- Translation on screen ("each, in an orbit, floating") is the creator's wording of
  the meaning, labelled as translation, not as Quranic text.

Tafsir (G6)
- Ibn Kathir, Tafsir al-Quran al-Azim, on 21:33: "qala Ibn Abbas: yaduruna kama
  yaduru al-mighzal fi al-falkah" (they revolve as the spindle turns in its whorl);
  Mujahid says the same. Ibn Kathir quotes it without a chain of narration (isnad),
  so it is presented as a report in his tafsir, not as a hadith.
  https://quran.com/21:33/tafsirs/ar-tafsir-ibn-kathir
  English abridgement: https://quran.com/21:33/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir

Science (G7), labelled "established science"
- Sun's orbital speed ~230 km/s and one orbit ~230 million years:
  NASA StarChild, "Question of the Month, February 2000" (828,000 km/h = 230 km/s,
  about 230 million years): https://starchild.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/StarChild/questions/question18.html
- Gaia-based measurement: Eilers, Hogg, Rix & Ness (2019), The Astrophysical
  Journal 871, 120, "The Circular Velocity Curve of the Milky Way from 5 to 25 kpc":
  229.0 +/- 0.2 km/s at the Sun (systematics ~5%). https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.09466
- Other estimates range ~230-240 km/s and 225-250 million years; the reel says
  "lagbhag" and "takreeban" and shows "~".
- 230 million years = 23 crore saal (1 crore = 10 million); checked.

Honesty claims (spoken, not graphics)
- "Arabic mein aur bhi palindromes hain": interpretation-neutral linguistic fact;
  e.g. the same phrase in 36:40 ("kullun fi falak").
- "Purane zamaane ke astronomers bhi maante the ki Sun ghoomta hai": Ptolemaic
  (geocentric) astronomy had the Sun moving on a circle; stated as history, and the
  reel says it is not a proof.
