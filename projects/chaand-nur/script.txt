Suraj abhi bujh jaaye, toh chaand bhi lagbhag *aath minute* mein bujh jaayega.
Kyunki chaand ki apni roshni hi nahi hai. Wo bas suraj ki roshni *lautata* hai.
Isi baat pe ek forward ghoomta hai: Quran ne 1400 saal pehle hi bata diya tha.
Saboot, Surah Nuh ki ye aayat: "wa ja'alal qamara fihinna nura, wa ja'alash shamsa siraja".
Chaand ko banaya *nur*. Aur suraj ko *siraj*, yaani jalta hua chiragh.
English translation mein likha hai, a "reflected" light. Par ruko, reflected *bracket* mein hai.
Arabic mein ye lafz hai hi nahi.
Aur nur ka matlab udhaar ki roshni ho bhi nahi sakta. Quran toh Allah ko bhi nur kehta hai.
Toh forward galat nikla? Poora nahi. Asli baat nur mein nahi, *siraj* mein hai.
Ye lafz poore Quran mein bas *chaar* baar aata hai.
Teen baar suraj ke liye. Ek baar Nabi sallallahu alaihi wasallam. Aur chaand ke liye? *Ek baar bhi nahi*.
Aur ye fark sahi baithta hai. Chiragh apna tel jalata hai, suraj apna fuel. Chaand kuch nahi jalata.
Haan, chaand ki roshni udhaar ki hai, ye Quran se *hazaar saal* pehle ek Greek shayar likh chuka tha.
Toh Quran ne koi nayi khoj nahi sunayi. Bas wo galti nahi ki, jo bahut aasaan thi.
Aasmaan mein chiragh ek hi hai. Aur Quran ne chiragh *usi* ko kaha.
Agli baar chaand dekho, toh ye yaad rakhna. Follow kar lo.
