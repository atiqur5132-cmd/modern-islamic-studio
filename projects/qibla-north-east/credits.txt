America mein qibla north-east kyun? — credits and sources
=========================================================

Quranic text
- 2:144, only the part "fa walli wajhaka shatra al-masjid al-haram" (cut by code from the
  fetched Ayah): api.quran.com v4 (text_uthmani), cross-checked with api.alquran.cloud
  (quran-uthmani) by scripts/fetch_ayah.py, 2026-11-08.
- English: Sahih International (quran.com translation 20).

History (full notes in research.md)
- S. Kamal Abdali, "The Correct Qibla", rev. 1997-09-17 (geomete.com/abdali/papers/qibla.pdf),
  quoting The Washington Daily News, 15 April 1953: the mosque "faces 56 degrees, 33 minutes,
  and 15 seconds east of true north"; passers-by: "Take any map… the line would go slightly
  south".
- Wikipedia, "Islamic Center of Washington": cornerstone 1949, completed 1954, dedicated 1957.
- Wikipedia, "Qibla" (citing King 1996, Bilici 2012, Bashori 2015): methods of Muslim
  astronomers from the 9th-10th centuries; most North American Muslims follow north-east,
  some mosques face east/south-east; facing the general direction (jihat al-ka'ba) accepted
  by many jurists.

Science and numbers
- Greenland and Africa: Wikipedia, "Mercator projection" ("Greenland appears the same size
  as Africa, when in reality Africa's area is 14 times as large"); areas 2,166,086 km²
  (Greenland) and 30,370,000 km² (Africa), Wikipedia, read 2026-11-09.
- Our own calculations (projects/qibla-north-east/make_maps.py and src/overlays/qibla/look.ts):
  great-circle bearing from the Islamic Center of Washington to the Kaaba 56.5° (1953 record:
  56°33′); from Anchorage 350.9°; the Washington-Makkah great circle crosses the Atlantic,
  France, southern Italy and passes near Crete (highest point 49.5° N); the Anchorage path
  passes within about 500 km of the North Pole (highest point 85.6° N).
