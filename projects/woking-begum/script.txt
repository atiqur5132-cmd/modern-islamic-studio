England mein ek masjid hai, jiska naam hai *Shah Jahan* Mosque.
Aur ye naam Mughal badshah ka nahi hai. Ye Bhopal ki ek *Begum* ka naam hai.
Kahani 1880s ki hai. England ke ek chhote se town Woking mein, ek scholar Gottlieb Leitner ek institute chalate the.
Wahan India aur Middle East se students padhne aate the.
Ab Muslim students namaz kahan padhein? England mein ek bhi building masjid ke liye nahi bani thi.
Toh Leitner ne madad maangi. Aur madad aayi *Bhopal* se.
Us waqt Bhopal par ek aurat ki hukumat thi, Nawab Shah Jahan Begum.
Unke donation se 1889 mein Woking mein ek chhoti si Mughal style masjid khadi ho gayi.
Lekin dus saal baad Leitner ki death hui, aur masjid par *taala* lag gaya.
Kuch saal baad Indian Muslims ne use dobara khulwaya.
Aur 1922 ki Eid par elaan hua, ki ab is masjid ka naam hoga, *Shah Jahan*.
Us Begum ke naam par, jinke paise se ye bani thi.
Haan, saara kharcha akele unhone nahi uthaya tha. Par naam unhi ka chala.
England ki zameen par khadi masjid. Neev mein Bhopal ka paisa, aur naam mein ek *aurat*.
India ki aisi aur kahaniyan sunni hain? Follow kar lo.
