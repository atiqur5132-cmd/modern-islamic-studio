# Woking's Shah Jahan Mosque and the Begum of Bhopal: research

Series B, Forgotten History. TOPICS.md #49. Told as one story in order: a scholar's
students need a place to pray → help comes from a woman ruler in Bhopal → the mosque is
built, closes, reopens → it takes her name. About 200 words, roughly 60 s (creator,
2026-11-12: continuity matters more than a 40 s cap).

**The one idea (for a 15-year-old):** England's first mosque built as a mosque is named
after a woman who ruled Bhopal, because her donation helped build it.

Sources accessed 2026-11-12.

## What the reel does NOT say

- Not "the first mosque in Britain" (Liverpool, 1887, converted house).
- Not "first in Europe" (Spain had mosques for centuries; S3's "Northern Europe" wording
  is a different claim).
- No sum of money; no claim she paid in full.
- No mention of the Ahmadiyya period (row 9).
