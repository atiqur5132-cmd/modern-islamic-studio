# Offa's dinar: research

Series B, Forgotten History. TOPICS.md #48. Target 30–40 s (creator, 2026-11-12), told
as one scene: an English engraver copying a coin he cannot read.

**The one idea (for a 15-year-old):** an English Christian king's gold coin carries the
kalima in Arabic, because the engraver copied an Abbasid dinar he could not read, and
put the king's name in upside down.

## What the reel does NOT say

- Not that Offa or England turned Muslim.
- Not that it is the only English coin with Arabic (S1 calls it unique as a dinar of
  Offa; other imitations exist in Europe).
- No claim about how the coin reached Rome or the museum (provenance unverified).

## Parked

- The mancus: the gold unit of account named in Offa's promise to Rome, linked to Abbasid
  gold coins in Francia (S3). A second idea, not for 40 s.
- Only three gold coins of Offa survive (S3).
