# Kahf Serial, Part 1: the youths who stood up

Series D (`SERIES.md`). Serial plan and series-level ledger: `../research.md`.
Written 2026-11-12. Status: script draft, not yet rehearsed or confirmed by the creator.

## The one idea

When the whole town stood on one side, a few young men stood on the other, and in the
cave they asked Allah for the right path, not for victory.

## Structure

A story told in order: hook (a Surah named after young men's hiding place) → who they
were (*fityah*) → the town against them → they stand up → they leave → what they asked →
for us → they fall asleep (cliffhanger: how long?) → CTA.

About 202 words, about 61 s at 3.3 words a second.
