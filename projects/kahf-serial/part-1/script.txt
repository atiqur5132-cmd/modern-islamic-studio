Kuch *jawaan ladke* ek gufa mein chhupe the.
Aur Quran ki ek poori Surah ka naam wahi gufa hai. Surah *Kahf*.
Is Surah mein chaar kahaaniyan hain. Har Juma ek. Aaj pehli.
Quran unhe kehta hai "fityah". Jawaan ladke.
Unke sheher mein sab log Allah ke alawa doosre khudaon ko poojte the.
Aur ye ladke sabke saamne *khade* ho gaye.
Bole, "Hamara Rabb wahi hai, jo aasmaan aur zameen ka Rabb hai."
Quran kehta hai, us waqt Allah ne unke dil *mazboot* kar diye.
Imam Ibn Kathir likhte hain, jawaan log sach ko jaldi maan lete hain.
Lekin poora sheher ek taraf tha, aur ye chand ladke doosri taraf.
Toh unhone sheher chhoda, aur gufa mein jaa baithe.
Gufa kahan thi, wo kitne the, Quran ye nahi batata.
Wo batata hai ki unhone wahan kya *maanga*.
Na jeet, na badla.
"Ae Rabb, apni taraf se rehmat de, aur hamare kaam mein *sahi raasta* bana de."
Bas sahi raasta.
Jab poora group ek taraf ho, aur sach doosri taraf, ye dua yaad rakhna.
Phir Allah ne unhe usi gufa mein *sula* diya.
