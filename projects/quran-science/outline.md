# Outline: Quran aur Science, tumhara ek din, 5 aayatein

First YouTube long video. **Draft 3 (2026-11-11):** the creator asked for a human scriptwriter's
voice that is easy to read as voiceover and told like a story. The video is now one journey through
the viewer's own day, morning to night. Each thing they touch or see (fingertip, water, iron,
ground, night sky) opens one verse. The ant opens the video and closes it. Earlier drafts:
`script.v1-claims.txt` (claim check, dropped: no critique of other videos) and
`script.v2-explainer.txt` (checklist style).

`script.txt`: 247 lines, 1,899 words, about **9½ min** at 3.3 words a second. The longest line is 18
words, and every line is one breath. This is a chapter map, not `beats.md`: visuals are planned after the take.

**Working title (English, for upload):** "Your Day in 5 Quran Verses — and What Science Adds"
**Thumbnail idea:** the creator on a rooftop at dusk, phone in hand, a red dot (Mars) in the sky,
and 3–5 words: "5 VERSES. ONE DAY."

- **Voice:** spoken to one friend ("tum"), present tense for the day scenes, one breath per line.
  Arabic is limited to five short words (banan, anzalna, sutihat, khunnas, kunnas) and two short
  recited lines. The three questions (word, first hearers, science) are introduced once (lines
  15–18). After that they are woven into the story, not called out as a checklist.
- **Honesty lines stay**, in the story's own voice and naming no one: the fingerprint verse is a
  promise, not a forecast (line 54); scholars differed on iron (line 118) and on the earth's shape
  (lines 159–165); the stars verse is hard to tie to black holes (lines 212–215).
- **Hook (0–6 s):** the creator is on screen from frame 0, with an ant macro beside them on line 1.
  Lines 9–13 (fingers, water, iron, ground, sky) each get a 1.5 s B-roll change.
- **Mid-video CTA:** line 138 (about 5:14, after the iron chapter): a YouTube subscribe animation, 2–3 s.
  Its line also hands over to the evening.
- **Comment ask (end card):** "Which part of your day should we read with the Quran next?"
- **Last line before the CTA (held 2 s):** "Kal subah jab alarm band karo, toh ungli ko ek pal dekh lena."
- **Claim tags:** science (NASA, NOAA, USGS, Wilson), forensic science (fingerprints), Quranic
  context (tafsir cards).
- **Render:** a 9½-minute render runs on GitHub Actions. Check the free minutes first.
