Tafsir
- Ibn Kathir on 55:19: the two seas are the fresh and salty waters, the fresh coming from rivers;
  the barrier is a barrier of land. Tabari on 55:20: barzakh is anything between two things
  (Qatada: dry land; Mujahid: a barrier from Allah). Scholars differ on what the barzakh is.
  (From projects/quran-science/research.md, Ch 2.)

Science
- The viral "two oceans" picture: Ken Bruland (UC Santa Cruz), Gulf of Alaska, 2007: glacial
  river silt meeting the ocean. "They do eventually mix." Alaska Dispatch News, Ben Anderson,
  5 Feb 2013. PolitiFact (2021) rated the "never mix" caption false.
- Gibraltar two-layer exchange: dense, salty Mediterranean water flows out along the bottom,
  lighter Atlantic water flows in on top; tides and turbulence mix the layers at the interface.
  Ocean Science 14, 1547 (2018), os.copernicus.org/articles/14/1547/2018/; NOAA estuary
  circulation tutorial (oceanservice.noaa.gov) for fresh water riding over salt water.
