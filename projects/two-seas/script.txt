Do samandar, beech mein ek *sharp line*, aur paani mix nahi hota. Ye viral video tumne zaroor dekha hoga.
Lekin ye Gibraltar nahi, *Gulf of Alaska* hai.
Jahan glacier ka mitti wala paani samandar se milta hai,
aur kuch der baad *mix* bhi ho jaata hai.
Toh kya Quran ki baat *galat* hui? Pehle dekho, Quran asal mein kehta kya hai.
Surah Rahman: "Usne do samandar chhod diye, jo aapas mein *milte* hain.
Unke beech ek barzakh, yaani *aad* hai. Wo ek doosre par hadd se nahi badhte."
Surah Al-Furqan khud ye clear kar deti hai ki ye kaun se do paani hain.
Ek *meetha* aur ek *khaara*. Imam Ibn Kathir bhi yahi kehte hain,
ki dariya ka meetha paani aur samandar ka khaara.
Aur yahi balance do samandaron ke beech bhi kaam karta hai.
Aap Gibraltar ki science dekho. Mediterranean ka paani zyada namkeen aur *bhaari* hai, isliye wo neeche se bahar nikalta hai.
Atlantic ka *halka* paani upar se andar aata hai.
Inke beech ki boundary ko *pycnocline* kehte hain, yaani density ki ek invisible layer.
Ye layer mixing ko rokti nahi, balki *dheema* karti hai.
Isliye "kabhi mix nahi hote" wala viral daawa *galat* hai.
Aur Quran ki aayat ye kehti bhi nahi.
Aayat kehti hai, koi paani doosre ko *hadd* se zyada nahi dabata.
Samandar ka namak dariya ko khatam nahi karta, aur hum usi *meethe paani* se jeete hain.
Isiliye bilkul agli aayat poochti hai, "Toh tum apne Rab ki kaun kaun si *nematon* ko jhutlaoge?"
