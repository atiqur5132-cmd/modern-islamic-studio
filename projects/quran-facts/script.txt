Kya tumhe pata hai, Quran mein *1400 saal* pehle insaan ki ungliyon ke *poron* ka zikr kiya gaya tha?
Surah Al-Qiyamah ki aayat number 3 aur 4 mein Allah Ta'ala farmate hain,
kya insaan yeh samajhta hai ki hum uski haddiyon ko jama nahi kar sakenge?
Kyon nahi? Hum to uski ungliyon ke *poron* tak ko theek karne par qaadir hain.
Ab zara gaur karo, aaj modern science jaanti hai ki har insaan ke *fingerprints* bahut distinctive hote hain.
Isiliye forensic science mein fingerprints ka istemaal kisi ki pehchaan establish karne ke liye kiya jaata hai.
Lekin yaad rakho, is aayat ka *asal context* Qayamat ke din insaan ko dobara zinda karna hai.
Aur yahi baat sochne par majboor karti hai.
Ek insaan ka jism kitni *hairat-angez* detail se bana hai.
Quran ko padho, us par gaur karo, aur uske paighaam ko samajhne ki koshish karo.
