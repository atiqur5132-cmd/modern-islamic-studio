Ek daana zameen mein gaya. Bahar aaye *saat sau*.
Ye kisi kisaan ka record nahi. Ye Surah Baqarah ki ek misaal hai.
Jo log Allah ki raah mein kharch karte hain, unki misaal ek *daane* jaisi hai.
Usse *saat* baaliyan niklin, aur har baali mein *sau* daane.
Saat guna sau. Diya ek, mila *saat sau*.
Par sau daane wali baali hoti bhi hai? Imam Tabari ne gyarah sau saal pehle khud ye poocha tha.
Unka jawab: ho toh wahi, warna ye ek *misaal* hai.
Lekin ye beej har jagah nahi ugta. Teen aayat baad, wahi beej, *doosri* zameen.
Ek chikni *chattan*, us par thodi si mitti.
Zor ki baarish aayi, aur chattan bilkul *saaf*.
Wo daana kaunsa tha? Jo dikhawe ke liye diya, ya dekar *jataaya*.
"La tubtilu sadaqatikum bil-manni wal-adha". Apna sadqa ehsaan jataakar aur takleef dekar *barbaad* mat karo.
"Maine hi toh tumhari fees bhari thi." Bas ek line, aur saari mitti *beh* gayi.
Agli aayat mein teesri zameen. Unchai par ek *baagh*.
Tez baarish mile toh *dugna* phal. Na mile, toh halki phuhaar bhi kaafi.
Wahan wo daana gira, jo sirf Allah ki *raza* ke liye diya gaya.
Daana sab daalte hain. *Zameen* sabki alag hoti hai.
Quran ki aisi aur misaalein samajhni hain, toh follow kar lo.
