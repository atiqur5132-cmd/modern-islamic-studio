## The one idea

eyes: lights that move, sometimes drift backwards, and hide at dawn. A black hole is never
seen at all.

Picture for it: two trains side by side. When yours is faster, the slower one looks as if
it is going backwards. Earth overtaking Mars works the same way.

## What the classical scholars said

**Tabari** lists three groups:

1. **The five bright planets.** In his words, they "draw back in their course and return, then hide in their houses as deer hide in their dens". He names them as Bahram (Mars), Zuhal (Saturn), Utarid (Mercury), al-Zuhara (Venus) and al-Mushtari (Jupiter).
   - Ali, by several chains (Khalid bin Ar'arah and others), said it means the stars that hide by day and appear at night. Al-Hasan, Qatada, Mujahid (in one report), Bakr bin Abdullah and Ibn Zayd said the same or close to it.
   - The grammarian al-Farra' (quoted in Tabari's notes and by Qurtubi) said: the five planets, "they go back in their course, and hide as deer hide in their den".
2. **Wild cows.** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud ("Wa ana ara dhalik"), Jabir bin Zayd and Ibrahim al-Nakha'i held this view. Ibrahim even said people were lying about Ali when they reported the stars view.
3. **Deer.** Ibn Abbas (one chain), Sa'id bin Jubayr and al-Dahhak held this view.

**Tabari's own view:** Allah swore by things that sometimes withdraw, sometimes run, and sometimes take shelter. Nothing in the verse limits it to stars, cows or deer, so it includes everything with those qualities. The den metaphor can be used for the places of the stars in the sky.

**Qurtubi:**
- He names the five planets "as the people of tafsir mention", reported from Ali.
- He quotes the lexical note that they are called khunnas "because they lag behind; they are the bewildered planets that go back and go straight".
- He gives the cows and deer views from Ibn Mas'ud, Jabir bin Abdullah and Ibn Abbas.
- He quotes al-Qushayri: "the most correct is to read it as the stars, because the night and the dawn are mentioned right after".
- He adds that Allah may swear by any creation, animal or object.

**Ma'ariful Quran** (widely read in India) takes the five-planets view and describes exactly the backward drift.

## The science

- **Retrograde motion:** Earth orbits faster than Mars. About every 26 months (780 days) Earth overtakes it. For roughly 72 days Mars seems to move backwards against the stars, then goes forward again. Mars never really reverses.
- **Other planets:** Jupiter and Saturn show it too, as outer planets. Mercury and Venus show it in their own way.
- **Known long before Islam:** Greek astronomers explained the backward drift with epicycles (Apollonius, 3rd c. BCE). So this is a description of the sky people could see, not a hidden discovery.
- **Black holes:** They give off no light and are found only by their effect on things around them. The first image (2019) shows glowing gas around the black hole, not the black hole itself.

## Parked (true, but not for this reel)

- The Prophet ﷺ recited 81:15–18 in the Fajr prayer (Sahih Muslim, via Ibn Kathir).
  - Fajr is the moment those lights fade, so it could become a closing reflection.
  - The hadith number needs checking at sunnah.com first.
- Ibn Kathir reads 'as'as (81:17) as the night approaching. Others read it as departing, and he says the word carries both meanings.
- "Planet" comes from the Greek for "wanderer", and the Arabic name is al-mutahayyira, "the bewildered ones". This is a nice parallel; check a dictionary before use.
- The black-hole videos read kunnas as "sweepers", from k-n-s "to sweep" (miknasa, broom). The root does carry that sense, but no lexicon was opened for it, so it stays out of the script.
