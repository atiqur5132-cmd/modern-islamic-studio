# Azaan ek sapne se shuru hui: research

Series B, Forgotten History (topic #71 in `TOPICS.md`). This is also the first reel in the
"why Muslims do this" strand the creator asked for on 2026-11-18.

**The one idea:** Muslims are called to prayer by a human voice saying words, not by a
bell or a horn. That choice was made in Madinah, and the words came through a Companion's
dream, which the Prophet ﷺ confirmed.

**Picture:** a bell that was about to be made, and what replaced it.

All hadith pages were opened on sunnah.com on 2026-11-18. Gradings are as shown on those
pages.

In the morning the Prophet ﷺ said: "It is a true vision, in sha Allah" (رُؤْيَا حَقٌّ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ). Teach it to Bilal, "for he has a louder voice than you" (أَنْدَى صَوْتًا مِنْكَ). ## Parked (true, but not this reel's one idea)

- Hook (draft 3, 2026-11-18): "Ek sapna na aata, toh aaj masjidon se ghanti bajti" is a
  counterfactual built on H4 (a bell was ordered to be made). It is rhetoric, not a claim; the
  next line states the record.

- Cut from the script for length (2026-11-18): the flag suggestion (row 2, H3 only) and
  Umar keeping the dream for twenty days (row 11). The script now says Umar "came running"
  (H4: dragging his cloak).

- How later scholars order H1 and H4 (Ibn Hajar in Fath al-Bari is said to read Bukhari's
  call as a general call before the dream). Not yet opened, so not used.
- The year (usually given as 1 AH / 622–623 CE; some say 2 AH). Not opened, not used; on
  screen say "first years in Madinah".
- Iqamah's words, the "as-salatu khayrun minan-nawm" addition at Fajr.
- Abdullah ibn Zayd ibn Abd Rabbih is known for this one hadith only (at-Tirmidhi's note
  under H5). Lovely for a pinned comment.

## Sound limits

No bell or horn sound, although the story is about both: Sahih Muslim 2114 calls the bell
"the musical instrument of Satan" (opened on sunnah.com 2026-11-18). Show them, give them
foley (set down, covered, a clapper caught), and let the first sound of a voice be the turn.
The real azaan recording, at the payoff, is the reel's one big sound (`SOUND.md`).

## Visual limits

Never show the Prophet ﷺ, Bilal, Umar, Abdullah ibn Zayd or any Companion, real or
AI-made. No film stills (for example from *The Message*, 1976). Use objects (a bell, a
horn, a flag), Madinah map, sound waves, the words of the adhan as type, and real present-day
masjid footage under a free licence.
