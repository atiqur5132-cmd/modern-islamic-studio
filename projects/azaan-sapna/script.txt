Ek sapna na aata, toh aaj masjidon se *ghanti* bajti.
Mazaak nahi kar raha. Madinah mein bell banwane ki baat *pakki* ho chuki thi.
Muslims naye-naye Madinah aaye the, aur namaz ke liye bulane ka koi tareeka hi nahi tha.
Toh ideas aaye. Koi bola, Jews ki tarah *horn* bajao. Koi bola, Christians ki tarah *bell*.
Nabi sallallahu alaihi wasallam ko dono pasand nahi aaye. Par isse behtar kuch tha bhi nahi.
Us raat Hazrat Abdullah ibn Zayd yahi fikr lekar soye.
Sapne mein ek aadmi dikha. Haath mein wahi *bell*.
Unhone kaha, ye mujhe bech do. Hum isse namaz ke liye bulayenge.
Wo bola, isse *behtar* cheez na bata doon?
Aur bell bajane ke bajaye, usne kuch *words* bolne shuru kiye. Wahi words, jo tum aaj din mein paanch baar sunte ho.
Subah Nabi ne sapna suna aur kaha, ye sacha sapna hai, in sha Allah. Ye words Bilal ko sikha do, unki awaaz *door tak* jaati hai.
Bilal ki pehli azaan sunte hi Hazrat Umar bhaagte hue aaye. Bole, maine bhi *yahi* sapna dekha hai.
Riwayaton mein details thodi alag milti hain. Par har riwayat ka ant ek hi hai: us din se Madinah ko ghanti ne nahi, ek *awaaz* ne pukaara.
Aur ye choice khaas kyun hai? Bell bas batati hai ki time ho gaya. Azaan batati hai ki aana *kyun* hai.
Usme ek line aati hai, Hayya 'alal-falah (حَيَّ عَلَى الْفَلَاحِ). Aao, *kaamyabi* ki taraf.
Ghanti sirf bula sakti thi. Azaan tumse *baat* karti hai.
Chaudah sau saal se ye awaaz din mein paanch baar tumhe bula rahi hai. Ek din bhi *thaki* nahi.
Agli baar suno, toh *jawab* dena. Aur ye kahaniyan tum tak pahunchti rahein, isliye follow kar lo.
