**Description:**
A viral post says the word "day" (yawm, يوم) appears exactly 365 times in the Quran, matching the days of a year. I checked it word by word.

The count, using the Quranic Arabic Corpus and cross-checked with the Tanzil text:
• All forms of yawm: 475
• Singular only: 445
• Without yawma'idhin ("that day"): 375
• Without yawmukum / yawmuhum ("your day / their day"): 365

So 365 is reachable, but only with one specific counting rule. Equally reasonable rules give other numbers. The Islamic (Hijri) calendar is lunar: 12 months × 29.53 days ≈ 354 days.

Many scholars caution against presenting selective word counts as a miracle. The Quran's own challenge is different: "produce a surah like it" (2:23).
