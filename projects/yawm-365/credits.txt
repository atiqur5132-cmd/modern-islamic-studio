Yawm 365 baar? A viral number claim, counted: sources and credits
====================================================================

Quranic text
- Not shown in the final reel (creator chose not to record the 2:23 lines). For the record:
  Surah Al-Baqarah 2:23, text_uthmani from api.quran.com v4, cross-checked with
  api.alquran.cloud (quran-uthmani) by scripts/fetch_ayah.py on 2026-11-05.
- Translation: Sahih International (quran.com translation id 20). The spoken line is the
  creator's Hinglish rendering of the meaning, labelled as translation.

Word counts (numerical observation, recomputed by us)

  corrected copy github.com/mustafa0x/quran-morphology, quran-morphology.txt.

  unchanged) via api.alquran.cloud/v1/quran/quran-simple-clean.
- Scripts: projects/yawm-365/rules.py and regex.py. Both data sets give 475 / 445 / 375 / 365.

Scholarly view

  Dr. Ashraf Abd al-Razzaq Qatanah. https://islamqa.info/en/answers/69741
