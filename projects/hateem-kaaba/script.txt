Ye Kaaba *poora* nahi hai.
Shock laga? Ye jo chhoti si half-circle wall hai na, ye bhi actually *Kaaba* hai.
Ise Hateem kehte hain. Hadith mein naam hai Hijr (الحِجْر).
Aur ye exact sawaal Hazrat Aisha ne Nabi sallallahu alaihi wasallam se poocha tha: kya ye wall bhi Kaaba hai?
Answer tha: *haan*.
Toh phir ye bahar kyun hai?
Kyunki jab Quraysh ne Kaaba rebuild kiya, beech mein unka *budget* kam pad gaya.
Aisha ne ek aur cheez notice ki. Darwaza itna *upar* kyun hai?
Reason: taaki Quraysh decide kar sakein, kaun andar jaayega aur kaun nahi. Full *control*.
Ab yahan interesting part aata hai. Nabi ke paas ye sab fix karne ka chance tha.
Kaaba ko Ibrahim alaihissalam ki original foundation pe le jaate, darwaza ground level pe le aate.
Par aapne nahi kiya. Kaha: tumhare log abhi abhi jahiliyat (الجَاهِلِيَّة), yaani ignorance ke daur se nikle hain. Darr hai, unke dil ye change *accept* na karein.
Ek wall se zyada, unhe logon ke *dil* ki care thi.
Bas ek detail: poora Hateem Kaaba nahi. Reports mein Kaaba wala part paanch-chhe haath ka hai, matlab lagbhag *do-teen metre*.
Phir bhi tawaf poore Hateem ke *bahar* se hota hai.
Aur bonus: Nabi ne Aisha se kaha tha, Kaaba ke andar namaz padhni ho, toh Hateem mein padh lo.
Wall *incomplete* reh gayi, taaki logon ka imaan *complete* rahe.
Koi Umrah pe jaa raha hai? Usse ye bhejo. Aur follow kar lo.
