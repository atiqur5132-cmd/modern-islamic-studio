## The three biggest visual decisions

1. **One drawn form tells the whole story.** A single ivory line drawing on the dark page grows
   step by step with the words: a drop, the drop clinging to a wall, a soft lump, a curled
   skeleton, then the skeleton clothed. Each Arabic word arrives beside the shape it names. It is
   plainly an illustration (labelled), never a fake embryo photo and no AI embryos. Real science
   pictures appear only where the creator states science: the implantation drawing (day 6–7) and
   a real ultrasound.
2. **Kasawnā said to camera.** Lines 12–13 become a top/bottom split: the creator above, the word
   [23:14 w10 fa-kasawna] large below. On "kapde" a soft rose layer is pulled over the bare skeleton like cloth
   from the shoulders down; on "joda nahi" the word *joined* fades and is struck through with a
   thin drawn line, and on "pehnaya" *clothed* lands. No stamps, no red cross.
3. **A dark, warm theme of its own.** Deep aubergine ground with ivory ink and one rose-clay
   accent that only ever marks the Quran's own word at each step (the Arabic word and the shape
   it names, including the flesh layer). Nothing like the Qibla reel's chart paper and orange.
