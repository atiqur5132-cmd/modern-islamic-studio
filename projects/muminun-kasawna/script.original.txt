Tum kabhi ek *boond* the.
Itni chhoti, ki aankh se dikhti bhi nahi.
Aur Surah Mu'minun tumhari ye poori kahani, ek hi verse mein sunati hai.
Pehle tum the "nutfah" (نُطْفَةً). Bas ek boond.
Phir wo boond ek jagah *chipak* gayi. Quran kehta hai "alaqah" (عَلَقَةً). Jo chipak jaaye.
Aur sach mein, chhathe-saatwe din embryo womb ki wall se chipak jaata hai.
Is word ka ek meaning blood clot bhi hai, aur Ibn Kathir ne yahi samjha.
Phir wo ban gayi "mudghah" (مُضْغَةً). Gosht ka chhota sa piece, bina kisi shape ke.
Phir usme bones bani, "izam" (عِظَـٰمًا).
Itne stages toh Greek doctor Galen bhi Quran se pehle likh chuke the.
Lekin ab aata hai mera favourite word.
"Fa-kasawnal izama lahma" (فَكَسَوْنَا). Humne bones ko gosht ke *kapde* pehnaye.
Allah ne *joda* nahi kaha. *Pehnaya* kaha.
Jaise maa apne bachche ko pyaar se kapde pehnati hai.
Science ke hisaab se bones aur muscles saath-saath bante hain. Toh ye timetable nahi, pyaar ki ek picture hai.
Aur phir, "khalqan akhar" (خَلْقًا ءَاخَرَ). Humne use ek bilkul *nayi* creation bana diya.
Ek boond se, ek insaan.
Wahi boond, aaj ye video dekh rahi hai.
Isliye verse kehta hai, "Fatabarakallahu ahsanul khaliqin" (أَحْسَنُ ٱلْخَـٰلِقِينَ). Allah bahut barkat wala hai, sabse best creator.
Agla Quranic word bhi saath mein samjhenge. Follow karke rakhna.
