Kya tumne woh viral video dekha hai, jismein *do samundar* milte hain, par unka paani mix nahi hota?
Surah Ar-Rahman ki aayat number 19 aur 20 mein Allah Ta'ala farmate hain,
usne do samundar chhod diye jo aapas mein milte hain, aur dono ke beech ek *parda* hai, jise woh paar nahi karte.
Surah Al-Furqan ki aayat 53 aur saaf batati hai, ek paani *meetha*, doosra *khaara*.
Ab science kya kehti hai? Jahan nadi samundar se milti hai, wahan meetha paani halka hone se upar rehta hai, aur khaara paani neeche.
Dono ke beech ek *boundary* banti hai, jahan namak achanak badal jaata hai. Ise *halocline* kehte hain.
Lekin poora sach yeh hai, ki yeh paani hamesha alag nahi rehte, *dheere dheere* mil jaate hain. Viral video wala paani bhi.
Mufassireen ne *barzakh* ke alag alag matlab bataye hain, isliye ise proof nahi, gaur karne ki baat samjho.
Quran padho, us par gaur karo, aur uske paighaam ko samajhne ki koshish karo.
